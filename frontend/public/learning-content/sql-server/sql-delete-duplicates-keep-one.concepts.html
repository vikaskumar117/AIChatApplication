THE IDEA

What is it?

A deterministic keeper rule selects exactly one surviving row per duplicate business key; other rows become deletion candidates.

WHY IT MATTERS

What problem does it solve?

A cleanup query without an explicit retention policy can delete the valuable or referenced record. Large unbounded deletes also create locks, log growth, and replication or availability impact.

UNDER THE HOOD

How does it work?

Preview ROW_NUMBER() candidates in a transaction, order by a documented preference plus a unique ID, check references and row counts, take a verified backup, then delete reviewed batches. The sample DELETE is intentionally commented out until preview, authorization, and rollback steps are complete.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

Remember

  • Never delete until the business key and keeper policy are explicit.
  • Use a deterministic unique tie-breaker in ROW_NUMBER ordering.
  • Preview, back up, account for references, and plan transaction-log impact.
  • Enforce uniqueness after cleanup to stop the source of duplicates.
