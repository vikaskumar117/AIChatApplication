THE IDEA

What is it?

Incident handling is a structured process to reduce customer impact, preserve evidence, communicate status, restore service, and prevent recurrence.

WHY IT MATTERS

What problem does it solve?

Uncoordinated debugging during an outage can increase blast radius, lose evidence, and delay a safe mitigation. A rollback or feature disable may be better than an immediate code fix.

UNDER THE HOOD

How does it work?

Declare severity and an incident lead, scope impact, check recent changes and SLOs, choose reversible mitigations, preserve relevant telemetry, communicate on a cadence, verify recovery, and run a blameless follow-up with owners and due dates.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

Remember

  • Restore service safely before attempting a risky deep fix.
  • Assign a clear incident lead and communications owner.
  • Use reversible mitigations and verify against customer-facing SLOs.
  • Post-incident review should produce owned, time-bound system improvements.
