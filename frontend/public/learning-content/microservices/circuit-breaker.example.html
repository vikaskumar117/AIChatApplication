Worked example

  1. Count only relevant failures, not caller cancellation or business rejection.
  2. Tune thresholds from dependency behavior and service objectives.
  3. Provide bounded probe traffic and monitor state changes.

TRADE-OFFS

Benefits and limitations

Benefits

  • Avoids wasting caller capacity on a failing dependency.
  • Allows a dependency to recover without a constant flood of requests.

Limitations

  • Thresholds can cause false opens or synchronized recovery bursts.
  • A breaker does not replace timeouts, retries, or business fallback.

DECISION GUIDE

When to use it

Use: Use for remote dependencies with observable failure signals and a defined fast-fail behavior.

Avoid: Avoid a breaker around local deterministic validation or as a substitute for fixing chronic capacity limits.

COMMON MISTAKES

Watch out

  • Avoid a breaker around local deterministic validation or as a substitute for fixing chronic capacity limits.
  • Assuming circuit breaker guarantees outcomes beyond its stated scope.

BEST PRACTICES

In practice

  • Compose breaker with a deadline and concurrency controls.
  • Make open-state behavior explicit and observable.

SENIOR-LEVEL SCENARIO

Production thinking

The shipping provider becomes slow. A breaker prevents every API worker from waiting on it; shipment creation remains pending and a background workflow resumes after recovery.

SUMMARY

In one minute

A circuit breaker limits repeated calls during failure; it is not a recovery mechanism by itself.
